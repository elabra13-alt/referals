## Brand & Style

This design system drives a two-sided commercial ecosystem linking physical commerce brands across Latin America with digital content creators on TikTok and Instagram. The visual language balances creator-driven viral momentum with fintech-grade transaction transparency. It avoids decorative fluff in favor of structural clarity, high-contrast layouts, and instant legibility.

### Visual Aesthetic & Philosophy
- **Flat Architectural Precision:** Strictly 100% flat, solid color fields. Gradients, artificial blurs, skeuomorphic volume, and textured sheen are banned across all views.
- **High-Velocity Contrast:** Stark contrasts between crisp white planes, deep slate backgrounds, and saturated solid primary accents create clear pathways for transactional operations.
- **Audience Resonance:** Caters equally to mobile-first content creators requiring high-energy validation metrics and enterprise retail executives demanding audit-level analytics.

## Colors

The palette operates entirely on flat, unblended planes. High-energy solid accents direct attention to critical financial triggers, campaign performance milestones, and conversion indicators without visual vibration or color bleeding.

### Color Tokens & Assignments
- **Primary Accent (`#FE2C55`):** TikTok Coral Solid. Reserved strictly for primary call-to-actions, instant conversion triggers, top-tier referral signals, and live campaign counters.
- **Secondary Accent (`#D62976`):** Instagram Berry Solid. Deployed for visual creator attribution, social platform routing, and secondary milestone achievements.
- **Success & Settlement (`#10B981`):** Fresh Mint Solid. Signifies confirmed payouts, positive revenue trajectories, and active brand approval stamps.
- **Information & Delivery (`#00B4D8`):** Cyan Solid. Designates tracking links, physical QR logistics checkpoints, and campaign sync status.
- **Attention & Holding (`#F59E0B`):** Solid Amber. Handles pending transactions, escrow limits, and creator contract approvals.
- **Surfaces:**
  - Base Light Surface: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for core content surfaces and interactive cards.
  - Secondary Light Surface: Crisp Light Slate (`#F8FAFC`) for view canvases, segregated panel backdrops, and inner metric blocks.
  - Dark Surface / Header Canopy: Deep Night Slate (`#0B0F17`) used for terminal states, analytics cards, and high-contrast summary sections.
- **Typography & Boundaries:**
  - High-Contrast Text: Deep Slate (`#0F141C`) ensures high-contrast readability against light backgrounds.
  - Subdued / Secondary Text: Muted Slate (`#64748B`) handles supporting labels, metadata, and data headings.
  - Crisp Structural Border: Flat neutral boundary (`#E2E8F0`) defines interactive modules, containers, and data grids.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes sharp structural hierarchy and metric clarity. Plus Jakarta Sans drives top-level headings and metric hubs with punchy geometric shapes. Inter provides clean legibility for dense commercial feeds, multi-line campaign briefs, and financial logs.

### Numerical & Currency Formatting Rule
All commercial metrics, currency formats (e.g., `$ MXN`, `$ USD`), conversion percentages, and analytics counters must apply CSS font-feature-settings: `"tnum" on, "zero" on`. This maintains alignment within balance sheets, modular performance cards, and ledger tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a modular 12-column grid for desktop views, adapting cleanly to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column layout on mobile. The spacing system uses an 8px base increment (with a 4px fine step) to align operational dashboards and creator mobile feeds.

### Structural Breakpoints
- **Mobile (`< 640px`):** Single-column stack with dynamic full-width edge containers; outer page margin fixed at `1rem` (`16px`).
- **Tablet (`640px - 1024px`):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) margins. Sidebar transforms into a persistent high-utility navigation bar.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12-column grid spanning a maximum content canvas of `1280px`. Left-aligned creator/brand control rail (fixed `260px`) accompanied by a dynamic modular card matrix.

### Alignment Discipline
Vertical rhythmic intervals between stacked visual cards are standardly pegged to `1rem` (`16px`), while section cluster transitions deploy `2rem` (`32px`). Components must avoid arbitrary pixel offsets to preserve alignment across tabular data rows.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids blurred drop shadows, ambient fuzz, and skeuomorphic gradients. Visual depth is established entirely through architectural layering, surface color contrast, and solid boundary lines.

### Depth Hierarchy via Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base (Tier 0):** Crisp Light Slate (`#F8FAFC`). Serves as the structural sub-surface for views, navigation backings, and viewports.
- **Primary Panels & Cards (Tier 1):** Solid Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a strict `1px solid #E2E8F0` frame. This structure clearly separates elements from the slate canvas.
- **Recessed Input & Well Surfaces (Tier 0.5):** Subdued Light Slate (`#F1F5F9`) enclosed by `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Highlights interactive input zones, filter strips, and data calculation fields.
- **High-Impact Overlays & Modal Floating Surfaces (Tier 2):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) framed by an intentional `2px solid #0F141C` high-contrast edge. 

### Focus and State Elevations
Hover and active states replace shadow spreads with border weight changes and solid color inversions. Elements never hover via artificial blur; instead, interactive items indicate elevation through crisp highlight boundaries (e.g., transition from `1px solid #E2E8F0` to `1px solid #FE2C55` or `1px solid #0F141C`).

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp boundary discipline with accessible interaction targets. The system standardizes on modern rounded corners (`0.5rem` / `8px` baseline; `1rem` / `16px` for outer card perimeters) paired with distinct `1px` or `2px` solid borders.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Outer Cards & Modular Containers:** `12px` to `16px` (`rounded-xl`), creating distinct, clean divisions between content cards.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** Uniform `8px` (`rounded-md`).
- **Badges, Tags, & Status Pills:** `9999px` (Full Pill) with solid color backgrounds or high-contrast borders.
- **Physical QR Code & Metric Frames:** Crisp `8px` with strict `1px solid #E2E8F0` outlines to frame operational scan targets cleanly.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action (Conversion / Payout Triggers):**
  - Solid TikTok Coral background (`#FE2C55`), Crisp White text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 700`. Zero shadow.
  - Border: None. Radius: `8px`.
  - Hover: Background changes to `#E02449`. Active: Background drops to `#C81E3F`.
- **Secondary Action (Brand Platform / Negotiation):**
  - Crisp White background (`#FFFFFF`), Deep Slate text (`#0F141C`), `1px solid #0F141C`.
  - Hover: Inverts to Deep Slate background (`#0F141C`) with Crisp White text (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Tertiary Utility:**
  - Crisp Light Slate background (`#F8FAFC`), Deep Slate text (`#0F141C`), `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
  - Hover: Border color shifts to `#0F141C`.

### Cards & Modular Data Units
- **Standard Modular Card:**
  - Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`). Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Border-radius: `12px` (`rounded-xl`).
  - Internal Padding: `16px` (Mobile) or `24px` (Desktop).
- **High-Impact Revenue / Dark Anchor Card:**
  - Background: Deep Night Slate (`#0B0F17`). Border: `1px solid #1E293B`.
  - Text: Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) with secondary metric labels in Muted Slate (`#64748B`). Accent points use Mint (`#10B981`) or Coral (`#FE2C55`).

### Chips, Tags & Status Badges
- **Status Indicator Badges:**
  - Compact height (`24px`), horizontal padding (`8px`), border-radius (`9999px`). Typography: `label-sm` (`font-weight: 700`, uppercase).
  - *Active Campaign:* Mint background (`#10B981`), Crisp White text (`#FFFFFF`).
  - *Pending Review:* Amber background (`#F59E0B`), Deep Slate text (`#0F141C`).
  - *Platform Identifier (TikTok):* Coral background (`#FE2C55`), Crisp White text (`#FFFFFF`).
  - *Platform Identifier (Instagram):* Berry background (`#D62976`), Crisp White text (`#FFFFFF`).

### Input Fields & Data Selectors
- **Form Controls:**
  - Height: `44px`. Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`). Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Border-radius: `8px`.
  - Text: Deep Slate (`#0F141C`), font-size: `14px`.
  - Placeholder: Muted Slate (`#64748B`).
  - Focus State: Border switches directly to `2px solid #FE2C55` (Coral) with zero glow or blur rings.

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Switches
- **Checkboxes & Radio Controls:**
  - Dimensions: `20px x 20px`. Border: `2px solid #E2E8F0`.
  - Selected State: Solid `#FE2C55` fill with a sharp white checkmark or center pip. Border color switches to `#FE2C55`.
- **Toggle Switches:**
  - Track: `44px x 24px`, solid `#E2E8F0`. Active Track: Solid `#10B981` (Mint). 
  - Knob: `18px x 18px` Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), solid, with zero drop shadow.

### Creator Commercial Link / QR Asset Hub
- Specialized dual-action component showcasing the creator's live referral link and the physical store's instant scannable QR code.
- Layout: Pure White panel with a `1px solid #E2E8F0` frame, containing an integrated high-contrast QR block on a flat `#F8FAFC` base, coupled with an inline tabular copy button and real-time commission rate badge (`$ MXN`).
